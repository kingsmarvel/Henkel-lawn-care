Lawn, landscape and snow services

Henkel is a full service outdoor maintenance and installation company. Our highly trained technicians handle jobs of all sizes for residential and commercial properties, and every estimate is free.

Lawn maintenance

Scheduled mowing, trimming and clean-up on a program that runs through the season.

Fertilization & weed control

Treatment programs that thicken the turf and keep weeds from taking hold.

Mulch installation

Fresh mulch laid through your beds to hold moisture and sharpen every edge.

Curbside leaf cleanup

Lose the bags and regain your free time. We vacuum the leaves straight from the curb.

Landscape installation

Design and build work that turns a plan on paper into a finished yard.

Paver patios & walkways

Patios, walks and steps built to sit level through Ohio winters.

Soil & fill delivery

Topsoil, fill dirt and stone dropped where you need it on site.

Snow removal

Plowing across a wide service area in Union, MO, day or night.

De-icing

Salting and treatment that keeps drives and walks safe underfoot.
