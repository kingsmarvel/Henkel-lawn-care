About us

A full service outdoor company

Henkel is the ideal lawn maintenance company for Union, MO customers looking for competitively priced work on their outdoor spaces. We're also a full-service landscape construction firm that brings even the most elaborate design ideas to life on residential and commercial properties.

After every project we ask our customers what they thought of the work — it's why 92% of them recommend us, and those comments are applied to every business decision made in our office.
